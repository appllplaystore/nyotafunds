NYOTA Fund Kenya: Understanding the Youth Empowerment Programme

A guide to NYOTA's objectives, eligibility requirements, business support, and official information channels.

The National Youth Opportunities Towards Advancement (NYOTA) Project is a Government of Kenya initiative financed by the World Bank. The programme aims to improve employment opportunities, support entrepreneurship and promote savings among young Kenyans.

Through a combination of business development support, workplace training, skills certification and financial literacy, NYOTA seeks to help young people develop sustainable income-generating opportunities.

Who is the programme intended to support?

The published general eligibility criteria target vulnerable young Kenyans who:

  • Have an education level of Form Four or below.
  • Are unemployed, underemployed or engaged in low-income work.
  • Meet the other requirements specified for the relevant programme intervention.

Eligibility does not automatically guarantee selection. Applicants must meet the applicable conditions and follow the official selection process.

Business development and start-up support

The programme includes business development training, mentorship, start-up capital support and links to relevant financial services. The published programme information describes business start-up capital of KSh 50,000 for successful beneficiaries, subject to the programme's requirements.

Is NYOTA registration free?

Yes. According to the official project information, participation is free of charge. Applicants should be cautious of anyone requesting payment in exchange for registration, guaranteed selection or the release of programme funds.
